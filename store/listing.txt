Drop. Pop. Combo. Collect!

Mochi Pop is a cozy block puzzle with the cutest blocks you've ever popped. Drag squishy mochi pieces onto an 8×8 board, fill rows and columns, and watch them pop in a wave of confetti.

★ EASY TO LEARN, HARD TO PUT DOWN
• Drag and drop pieces with one thumb. No timer, no rush.
• Fill a row or column to clear it. Clear several at once for big points.
• Every tray is guaranteed to have a move, so a bad run never feels unfair.

★ COMBOS AND FEVER
• Keep clearing lines to build a combo streak with rising chimes.
• Fill the Fever meter for 8 moves of double points and coins on a rainbow board.
• Sweet Lines: clear a line of one colour for a bonus.

★ COLLECT MOCHI FRIENDS
• Spend coins at the Capsule Machine to unlock Kitty, Teddy, Bunbun, Chirp, Froggy, Panpan and Blip.
• Your chosen friend changes every block on the board.

★ SOMETHING NEW EVERY DAY
• A 7-day login calendar with bigger treats each day.
• Three daily missions with coin rewards.
• Level up for free boosters: hammer, bomb and new pieces.

★ PLAY YOUR WAY
• Plays offline. Your board is saved after every move.
• Sound, music and vibration each have their own switch.
• Optional reward videos for free coins or a second chance. Remove Ads is available as a one-time purchase.

Pop a few mochis on your coffee break, or chase a new high score. Have fun!
