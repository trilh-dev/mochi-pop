Mochi Pop: Cute Block Puzzle

SHORT DESCRIPTION (80 max)

FULL DESCRIPTION (4000 max)
Mochi Pop is a relaxing block puzzle game with the cutest blocks you've ever popped. Drag squishy kawaii mochi pieces onto an 8×8 board, fill rows and columns to clear lines, and watch them pop in a wave of confetti. Simple to learn, satisfying to master, and fully playable offline.

★ CLASSIC BLOCK PUZZLE, MADE ADORABLE
• Drag and drop block pieces with one thumb. No timer, no rush.
• Fill a row or column to clear it. Clear several lines at once for big points.
• Every tray is guaranteed to have a move, so a bad run never feels unfair.
• A calm brain game that trains your planning and spatial skills.

★ COMBOS AND FEVER
• Keep clearing lines to build a combo streak with rising chimes.
• Fill the Fever meter for 8 moves of double points and coins on a rainbow board.
• Sweet Lines: clear a line of one colour for a bonus.
• Clear the whole board for a huge score.

★ COLLECT CUTE MOCHI PETS
• Spend coins at the Capsule Machine to unlock Kitty, Teddy, Bunbun, Chirp, Froggy, Panpan and Blip.
• Your chosen friend changes every block on the board.

★ SOMETHING NEW EVERY DAY
• A 7-day login calendar with bigger treats each day.
• Three daily missions with coin rewards.
• Level up for free boosters: hammer, bomb and new pieces.

★ OFFLINE GAME, NO WIFI NEEDED
• Play anywhere, on the bus or on a plane. Your board is saved after every move.
• Small download, smooth on older phones.
• Sound, music and vibration each have their own switch.
• Optional reward videos for free coins or a second chance. Remove Ads is a one-time purchase.

If you love block games, brick puzzles, match and line-clear games or anything cute and cozy, Mochi Pop is your new favourite time killer. Pop a few mochis on your coffee break, or chase a new high score. Have fun!
