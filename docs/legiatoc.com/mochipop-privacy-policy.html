Privacy Policy

Mochi Pop: Block Puzzle — Android
Developer: sillygoose (Van Lang Technologies Company Limited)
Last updated: October 7, 2026

Mochi Pop is a block-puzzle game operated by Van Lang Technologies Company Limited, Long Bien, Hanoi, Vietnam ("we"). Contact: admin@legiatoc.com.

1. What stays on your device

Your scores, coins, boosters, collected skins, settings and the board of the game in progress are saved only on your device. We have no account system and no server that receives this information. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows optional reward videos and, between some games, full-screen ads. These are served by Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, prevent fraud and, where you allow it, personalise ads, Google may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address (and the approximate location derived from it), device and app information, and how you interact with ads. Google processes this data under its own policy: How Google uses information from apps that use its services.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy options in the game. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your device's Android settings.

Buying Remove ads stops the ads between games. Reward videos stay available but are only shown when you choose to watch one.

3. In-app purchases (Google Play)

Coin packs and Remove ads are sold through Google Play Billing. Payment is handled entirely by Google; we never see your payment details. The app receives only a purchase confirmation from Google Play so it can deliver what you bought.

4. Game updates

When the app starts, it checks GitHub (raw.githubusercontent.com) for a newer version of the game and downloads it if there is one. Like any web request, GitHub sees your IP address. No personal information is sent.

5. Sharing and retention

We do not sell or share personal information. Apart from the Google services described above, no data leaves your device. We keep no data about you on our side.

6. Children

Mochi Pop is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

7. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
